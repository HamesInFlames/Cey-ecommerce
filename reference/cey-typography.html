the type system

1 · signature
c.ey artist shop

Ms Madi · 400 · display only, 42–96px
Stands in for the hand-drawn marker logo. Use for the shop name, hero headline, and flourish words (“thank you”, “new!”). For the real site, export the actual drawn signature as an SVG and keep this face as its text fallback — a font can echo a signature, but the original scrawl is the brand.

2 · handwriting ui
c a t a l o g u e   ·   a b o u t   ·   e v e n t s

Gochi Hand · 400 · nav, headings, buttons, labels · 13–26px
Always lowercase. Nav links get the wide .38em tracking (the mockup’s signature move); buttons and product names get the tighter .22em. Never use below 13px — handwriting collapses when small.

3 · pen notes

Annie Use Your Telescope · 400 · prices, marquee strips, footer asides · 15–28px
The tall, skinny pen-writing from the mockup’s price tags. Keeping prices in a different hand than the labels is what makes the page feel genuinely handwritten instead of “handwriting font applied everywhere”.

4 · workhorse
Each sticker is printed on weatherproof glitter vinyl, roughly 6 cm tall, and ships in a rigid mailer within 3–5 business days. Orders over $25 ship free within Canada.

Zen Maru Gothic · 400 / 500 / 700 · paragraphs, product descriptions, forms, cart, checkout, policies · 12.5–16px
A rounded Japanese “maru gothic” — the typeface family of cute stationery packaging — so body text stays on-aesthetic while being fully readable. All ecommerce-critical text (prices in the cart, totals, shipping forms, error messages) lives here, never in handwriting. Bonus: it covers Japanese glyphs if product names ever need them.

house rules

  1. Lowercase is law for Gochi Hand — the moment something is capitalized, it stops looking like the mockup.
  2. Tracking = hierarchy. Wide (.38em) means navigation, tight (.22em) means labels, none means notes. Don't invent in-between values.
  3. One hand per job. Script for identity, Gochi for UI, Annie for prices/notes. If two handwriting faces appear in the same line, something's wrong.
  4. Handwriting is decoration, Zen Maru is function. Anything a customer must read to complete a purchase (checkout, totals, addresses, errors) uses the workhorse.
  5. Minimum sizes: Gochi 13px, Annie 15px, Zen Maru 12.5px. Below that, switch to Zen Maru.
